## Overview

The first week focused on integrating into the development team and establishing a productive local environment.

### Key Activities

- **Team Introduction** — Met with the project lead and fellow interns; reviewed the team's Agile workflow on Jira.
- **Environment Setup** — Installed Node 20 LTS, configured ESLint + Prettier, and set up Git hooks with Husky.
- **Codebase Walkthrough** — Explored the monorepo structure, understood the CI/CD pipeline, and mapped component dependencies.

### Challenges

Setting up the legacy database locally required a custom Docker Compose configuration. Documented the steps in the team wiki for future interns.

### Takeaways

> Starting strong with documentation pays dividends. The setup guide I wrote saved two other interns hours of debugging.
